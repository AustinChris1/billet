# Billet: invoice and receipt page, landing

Scope: the billet page (/b, one invoice that is also its receipt; Operate inside a Persuade frame for judges) and the landing (/, Persuade). The issue flow (/new) inherits this world.
Audience and job: a payer checks an invoice and pays it in OUSD; an issuer seals an invoice and shares a link; a judge opens two sample links and sees one invoice each.
Proof on hand: a real delivery proof verified in the browser against Zcash mainnet; a real Tempo TransferWithMemo whose memo is the billet id.
Constraints: no fabricated users or claims; say what is public (Tempo amount) and what is private (invoice text).
